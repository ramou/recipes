# Gravy Recipe

## Ingredients:
### Base
- 1.5 cups meat broth (turkey or chicken recommended, but whatever meat you're serving, basically)
- 1/2 cup total liquid (milk) for thickening
- 4 tsp all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper

### Herbs (default for turkey)
- A pinch of each:
  - Basil
  - Sage
  - Thyme

## Instructions:
01. In a small saucepan, bring 1.5 cups meat broth to a boil.
02. In a separate bowl or cup, stir together the cold milk and broth (totaling 1/2 cup) with 4 tsp of flour. Mix until smooth and free of lumps.
03. Remove the broth from heat. Slowly pour in the flour mixture, stirring constantly to prevent clumping.
04. Return to low heat and add:
    - 1 tsp salt
    - 1/4 tsp black pepper
    - A pinch each of basil, sage, and thyme (see variations below)
05. Simmer the gravy gently for at least 10 minutes, stirring occasionally until thickened and flavourful.

## Notes:
- Flour does not scale linearly with meat broth volume, adjust based on desired thickness.
- For a smoother gravy, strain before serving if needed.

## Spicing Variations:
### For Pork Gravy:
- Suggested herb combo:
  - 1/2 tsp each of:
    - Sage
    - Marjoram
    - Fennel seed (lightly crushed)
    - Thyme

### For Beef Gravy:
- Replace default herbs with:
  - 1/2 tsp each of:
    - Marjoram
    - Rosemary
    - Savory
    - Basil


---

*Tip: You can boost umami by adding a splash of soy sauce or Worcestershire sauce for beef or pork versions.*
