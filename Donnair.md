## Donair (Pork Version)

### Ingredients

#### Meat
- 1 kg Ground Pork
- 10 g (2 tsp) Salt
- 5 g (2 tsp) Onion Powder
- 5 g (2 tsp) Garlic Powder
- 3 g (1 tsp) Smoked Paprika
- 3 g (1 tsp) Black Pepper
- 1 g (1/4 tsp) Cayenne Pepper (optional)
- 3 g (1 tsp) Dried Oregano
- 3 g (1 tsp) Dried Thyme
- 15 g (2 Tbsp) Breadcrumbs (optional, for texture)

#### Donair Sauce
- 300 ml Sweetened Condensed Milk
- 75 ml (≈75 g) White Vinegar
- 2 g (1 tsp) Garlic Powder

### Directions

#### Meat Preparation
1. **Mix the spices**: In a large bowl, combine all the dry spices.
2. **Prepare the meat**: Add the spice mixture to the ground pork and knead thoroughly for 3–5 minutes until fully combined.
3. **Shape the loaf**: Form the mixture into a compact loaf shape, ensuring it holds together well.

#### Cooking (Air Fryer)
1. **Preheat** the air fryer to 190°C (375°F).
2. **Cook the meat**: Place the loaf on a piece of foil or directly in the air fryer basket.
3. **Air-fry** for 20–25 minutes, flipping halfway through, until the internal temperature reaches 71°C (160°F).
4. **Rest and slice**: Let the loaf rest for 5 minutes before slicing thinly against the grain.

#### Donair Sauce
1. **Whisk** the vinegar slowly into the condensed milk until it thickens.
2. **Add garlic powder** and mix thoroughly.
3. **Chill** the sauce for at least 15 minutes before serving.

### Assembly
1. **Warm the pita**: Use your naan-style pita bread.
2. **Layer** the sliced donair meat, diced tomatoes, and onions.
3. **Drizzle** generously with donair sauce.
4. **Wrap and serve** while warm.

*Tip*: For crispier meat, briefly pan-fry the sliced donair meat before serving.
