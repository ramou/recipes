# Quiche Recipe (Metric)

## Ingredients
- **1 savoury pie crust**
  - For a 10" pie shell (for a 9" shell, halve the eggs, milk, and basil, and reduce the onion/ham slightly)

- **Filling:**
  - 6 large eggs (300 g)
  - 750 ml heavy cream (or substitute with soy/almond/regular milk)
  - 200 g Gruyère/Emmental cheese (crumbled)
  - 150 g Black Forest ham, minced
  - 150 g caramelized onions (reduce liquid for best results)
  - 30 g sun-dried tomatoes (or substitute with roasted red peppers, semi-dried tomatoes, or sautéed mushrooms)
  - 5 g dried basil (1 tsp)
  - 5 g salt (1 tsp)

---

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the Crust:**
   - Blind bake the savoury pie crust at 180°C (355°F) for 10–15 minutes, or until lightly golden. Set aside.

2. **Preheat the Oven:**
   - Preheat to 150°C (300°F).

3. **Prepare the Egg Mixture:**
   - Whisk together the eggs, cream (or milk), salt, and basil until smooth.

4. **Layer the Ingredients:**
   - Sprinkle half of the cheese evenly across the bottom of the pie crust.
   - Alternate layers of ham and caramelized onions on top of the cheese:
     - Start with a layer of ham, followed by onions, and repeat (ham/onion/ham/onion).
   - Add the sun-dried tomatoes (or your chosen substitute) evenly over the layers.

5. **Combine and Top:**
   - Pour the egg mixture carefully over the layered ingredients.
   - Sprinkle the remaining cheese evenly on top.

6. **Bake:**
   - Place in the oven and bake for 60 minutes, or until the quiche is set in the middle and lightly golden on top.

---

## Notes
- **Substitutes for Sun-Dried Tomatoes:**
  - Roasted red peppers: Sweet and smoky, similar texture.
  - Semi-dried tomatoes: Slightly juicier than sun-dried.
  - Sautéed mushrooms: Adds a rich, earthy flavour.

- **Cheese and Filling Variations:**
  - Swap Gruyère/Emmental for cheddar, feta, or goat cheese.
  - Use cooked spinach, leeks, or sautéed zucchini for vegetarian options.

- **Layering Tip:**
  - Cheese on the bottom and top helps prevent a soggy crust, while layering the filling keeps the texture consistent.
