# Fajita Sauce

* 2 tsp Cornstarch
* 1 tsp Chili powder (the spice blend)
* 3/4 tsp Smoked paprika
* 1/2 tsp Ground cumin
* 1/2 tsp Garlic powder
* 3/4 tsp Salt
* 1/2 tsp Sugar
* Pinch Cayenne pepper (optional)
* 1/2 tsp white wine vinegar
* 1/2 cup (125 mL) water


Mix the dry ingredients together, then:
1. Brown your sliced meat in a tablespoon of oil.
2. Add sliced peppers and onions.
3. Stir in the seasoning with 1/2 cup (125 mL) water.
4. Simmer uncovered for 4-6 minutes, until the sauce thickens and coats everything.
5. Stir in 1/2 tsp white wine vinegar near the end of cooking. Taste and increase to 1 tsp if needed.
